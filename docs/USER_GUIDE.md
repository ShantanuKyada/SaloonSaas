# User guide

How to do the everyday jobs. Each section is written for one role; owners can do everything below. Screens only show what
your role allows, so you may not see every item mentioned here.

## The five things everyone should know

1. **Press `N` for a new appointment, `W` for a walk-in, `/` to find anyone, `?` to see every shortcut, `Esc` to close.**
   Shortcuts never fire while you are typing in a box.
2. **The branch name at the top** (when you have more than one) decides which branch you are looking at. Times are always
   shown in that branch's time.
3. **Nothing is lost by mistake.** Cancelling an appointment can be undone from the message that appears, and a cancelled
   appointment can be restored. Bills and payments are never deleted: they are voided or adjusted with a reason, so the
   history stays honest.
4. **If something cannot be done, the message says why and what to try** (for example "Amit is already booked from 3:00 PM to
   3:45 PM. Choose another time or team member.").
5. **A pending amount never blocks a booking.** It is shown as a reminder so you can collect it when the customer visits.

## Receptionist: the front desk

### Book an appointment (about 20 seconds)

1. Press `N` (or **New appointment**).
2. **Customer:** type a name, mobile number or customer code. New customer? Choose *Add “…” as a new customer*: a name and
   mobile number are enough.
3. **Services:** tap what they want. Their usual services and the most-booked ones come first.
4. **When and who:** pick the day, then a time. Only times that can really be booked are listed. If several people are free
   you will see who; the customer's usual stylist is suggested.
5. **Confirm booking.** A confirmation message goes to the customer if they agreed to messages.

Booking several services in a row? Tick them all; they are scheduled back-to-back. If one person cannot do all of them,
switch on *Different team members for each service*.

### Walk-ins

Press `W`, find or add the customer, tap the services. Either choose a team member and *Start the service right now*, or *Add to
queue* and assign someone later from **Walk-ins** (the board shows each person's waiting time).

### When the customer arrives and is served

Open the appointment (from the front desk list, the calendar or the customer's page): **Check in → Start service → Complete
service → Create bill**. *More actions* has *Reschedule*, *Cancel* and *Mark as no-show*.

### Bill and take payment

1. **Create bill** from a completed appointment (or `B` for a bill without one). The services are filled in.
2. Offers that apply are added automatically; you can choose another, type a code, or give a manual discount with a reason.
3. **Payment:** choose Cash, UPI, Card or Bank transfer. Several ways at once? *Split payment*. The customer will pay later?
   Switch on *Pay later (credit)* and the amount stays pending on their account.
4. **Create bill and take payment.** On the bill page, *Print receipt* or *Share on WhatsApp*.

Paid advance, or more than the bill? The extra is kept on their account as an advance and used next time.

### Collect a pending payment

`P` (or **Billing → Collect payment**): choose the customer, enter the amount received, pick the method. By default the oldest
bills are settled first; you can choose specific bills. **Pending payments** lists everyone who owes money, oldest first, with
a one-tap *Remind* button (reminders are limited so customers are not pestered).

## Branch manager: the day at a glance

* **Today at the salon** shows who is free, serving, on a break or away right now, how many are waiting, today's sales and what
  needs attention. It refreshes by itself. "Free" always means free *and* the salon is open; just-finished services show clean-up
  time.
* **Calendar:** one column per team member. Drag an appointment to move it or drag its bottom edge to change its length; you are
  asked to confirm and can notify the customer. Grey hatching is time off, leave or outside working hours.
* **Team → Schedules & leave:** weekly hours and breaks, one-day changes (an extra Sunday), leave (you are shown any bookings
  that now need a new team member), and blocked time (training, supplier visit).
* **Team → Attendance:** who came in and when. Forgot to punch? *Add missing punch* or *Correct* the day; both ask for a reason
  and are recorded.
* **Team → Assign services:** one grid of who can do what. A box is greyed out when the person lacks the skill the service needs.

## Owner: setting up and steering

* **First time:** *Settings → Setup guide* walks through the salon details, branches, services, team, skills, working hours and
  messaging. Each step opens the real screen, and ticks appear by themselves.
* **Dashboard:** choose the period (Today, This week, This month …). Revenue, appointments, customers served, pending payments,
  branches side by side, top services and customers. Orange and blue cards are things to act on (overdue payments, a quiet
  tomorrow, a team member with no services).
* **Services:** price, time, category, who can do it, which branches. *Packages & combos* and *Offers* (percentage or fixed,
  dates, days of the week, first-visit, birthday, loyalty, usage limits).
* **Customers → Customer groups:** describe who you want ("not visited in 60 days"), see the count change as you type, then
  **Campaigns** to message them. Only customers who agreed to offers are ever contacted.
* **Reports:** pick a report, a date range and filters; view it, print it or *Download for Excel*. Everything on screen is also
  in the file.
* **Users & roles:** add logins, choose branches, and tailor what each role can do. Temporary passwords must be changed at first
  sign-in. Switching a person off signs them out immediately. The *Two-step sign-in* column shows who has it on, and
  **Reset two-step sign-in** in a person's menu switches it off for someone who lost their phone (see below).
* **Activity log:** who changed what and when, with before and after values (prices, settings, bills, attendance corrections,
  downloads).
* **Settings:** appointment slot length, booking window, tax and rounding, bill numbering, reminders and quiet hours, what team
  members can see, and **Connections** for WhatsApp, email, SMS and attendance machines (keys are stored encrypted and never
  shown again).

## Everyone: two-step sign-in (a code from your phone)

Two-step sign-in asks for a 6-digit code from an app on your phone after your password, so a stolen or guessed password alone
cannot open your account. Anyone can turn it on; owners decide whether it is **required** (*Settings → Privacy → Two-step
sign-in*: optional for everyone, required for owners, or required for owners and managers). We recommend at least
*Required for owners*.

**Turn it on (about a minute).** Open the round account button at the top right, choose **Account security**, then
**Turn on two-step sign-in**.

1. Install an authenticator app on your phone: Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, Authy or 1Password.
2. In the app, add an account and scan the square code on the screen. If you cannot scan it, choose "enter a setup key" in the
   app and type the key shown under the code.
3. Type the 6-digit code the app shows now and press **Turn on**.
4. **Save your recovery codes.** You get ten codes. Each lets you sign in **once** if you lose your phone. Copy them or download
   the file, keep them somewhere private (a password manager or a printed copy in a safe place), tick the box, and press
   **Done**. They are shown only once.

If your salon requires it, you will be taken to this screen as soon as you sign in and the rest of the app stays locked until
it is on.

**Signing in.** Enter your email or mobile number and password as usual, then the 6-digit code from your app. The code changes
every 30 seconds and works once, so if one is refused wait for the next. Five wrong codes lock the account for 15 minutes,
just like five wrong passwords.

**Lost your phone?**

* *You have a recovery code:* on the code screen choose **Use a recovery code instead**, type one (capitals and the dash do not
  matter), and then go to **Account security → Make new recovery codes** (and, when you have a new phone, turn two-step
  sign-in off and on again to link it).
* *You have no recovery codes:* ask your owner. In **Users & roles**, open the menu next to your name and choose **Reset
  two-step sign-in**. You are signed out everywhere and sign in with just your password; if two-step sign-in is required you
  set it up again straight away. If you are the only owner, whoever looks after the server can run
  `USER_EMAIL=you@salon.example npm run user:reset-2fa -w @salon/api`.

**Other things on this screen.** *Make new recovery codes* replaces all the old ones, so use it when only a few are left or if
someone may have seen them (it asks for your password and a current code). *Turn off two-step sign-in* also asks for both, and
is not offered if your salon requires it for your role.

## Stylist: My day

Signing in shows only your appointments for today: the one in progress, the next one, and the rest. Tap **Start service** and
**Complete service**. You can see the customer's notes but only the contact details your salon allows.

## Customers: booking online

Share `https://your-salon-address/book/<salon-code>` (see *Settings → Booking*). The customer chooses a branch and services, a day
and a time that is really free, and enters a name and mobile number. They receive a private link to **change the time** or
**cancel** (not within two hours of the appointment: they are told to call instead). No account or password is needed.

## When something does not work

| You see | Usually means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "This is outside opening hours" | The branch is closed then, or it is a holiday | Choose another time; managers can allow special hours from the booking screen |
| "Nobody is set up to do all of these services together" | No one has every skill needed | Book them one at a time, or ask a manager to assign the service (Team → Assign services) |
| A time you expected is missing | The person is on leave, on a break, blocked, or the time is too soon (minimum notice) | Check *Schedules & leave*; the booking screen shows each person's status in words |
| "Over the credit limit" | The customer's pending amount would pass their limit | Collect some payment now; a manager can approve going over |
| You cannot find a page | Your role does not include it | Ask the owner or manager to change your role |
| A message "could not be sent" | The messaging connection needs attention | *Messages → Delivery* shows the reason and a *Try again* button; owners check *Settings → Connections* |
| "That code didn't work" on sign-in | The code expired (they change every 30 seconds), was already used, or the phone's clock is wrong | Wait for the next code and type it without delay; check the phone's date and time is set automatically; or use a recovery code |
| "Set up two-step sign-in to continue" | Your salon requires it for your role and it is not on yet | Follow the steps on the screen; it takes about a minute |

Forgot your password? Ask your owner or manager to set a temporary one.
